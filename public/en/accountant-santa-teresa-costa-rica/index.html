Accountant in Santa Teresa, Cóbano, Costa Rica

We serve hospitality, tourism, restaurant and real-estate businesses, and foreign property owners on the Nicoya Peninsula. Our Santa Teresa office is across from the soccer field, on the second floor of Súper El Mango, and CPA Gustavo Monge A. visits it on a scheduled basis every month.

Services for Santa Teresa and the peninsula

  • Bookkeeping and tax returns for tourism and hospitality businesses
  • Taxes on short-term rentals and capital gains on property sales
  • Company formation and catch-up of existing companies
  • CPA certifications and reports for banks
  • Service and reports in English and Spanish

How we serve Santa Teresa

In-person visits are scheduled during the month and, on first-week visits, we also collect clients' documents. Message us on WhatsApp for the next dates and to set up your meeting. Between visits we work by video call, e-mail and WhatsApp.
