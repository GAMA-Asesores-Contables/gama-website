Accounting, Tax and Audit Services in Costa Rica

GAMA Asesores Contables is a Costa Rican firm with offices in Barranca de Puntarenas and Santa Teresa de Cóbano. These are the services CPA Gustavo Monge A. and his team provide.

CPA certifications

Income, financial statement and balance certifications issued by a Costa Rican Certified Public Accountant (CPA) under Law 7527. Barranca and Santa Teresa.

Audit and assurance

External audit and independent assurance by a CPA under International Standards on Auditing. Reports for banks, partners and investors.

IFRS for SMEs

IFRS for SMEs implementation and financial statement preparation: gap analysis, accounting policies, comparative statements and notes.

Tax compliance

Income tax, VAT and withholding returns on TRIBU-CR, capital gains and tax planning for companies, professionals and foreign owners.

Accounting outsourcing

Monthly bookkeeping, reconciliations, e-invoicing and payroll without growing your headcount. Reports in English and Spanish.

Financial projections

Projections, cash flow forecasts and scenarios to decide on investments, credit and growth, with clear, documented assumptions.

Company formation

Accounting and tax support to set up an S.R.L. or S.A. in Costa Rica: tax registration, initial obligations and bookkeeping from day one.
