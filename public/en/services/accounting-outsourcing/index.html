Accounting Outsourcing for Companies in Costa Rica

Keep your books up to date with a team supervised by a CPA, without hiring or training in-house staff. We record, reconcile and report every month so you see your numbers on time and meet your obligations.

What is included

  • Monthly bookkeeping and closing
  • Bank and electronic-invoice reconciliations
  • Control of received electronic documents
  • CCSS payroll and salary withholdings
  • Monthly financial statements and management report
  • Bilingual reports for owners living abroad

When it makes sense

It helps if your company is growing and bookkeeping is eating your time, if you have no staff with accounting judgment, or if you own a business from abroad and need reliable information. Each client follows a clear monthly calendar for document delivery and reports.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to send every month?

Issued and received invoices, bank statements, payment receipts and payroll information. We give you the deadlines for each month.

How does it work if my business is in Santa Teresa?

You can send documents digitally at any time, and we also collect documents and set up meetings during our scheduled in-person visits to Santa Teresa.

Who reviews the work?

A team of accounting assistants supervised by CPA Gustavo Monge A.
