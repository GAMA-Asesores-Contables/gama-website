Company Formation: Accounting and Tax Support

Setting up a company in Costa Rica has a legal side, handled by a notary public, and an accounting and tax side that is best planned from the start. We take care of the second part and coordinate with your attorney or notary.

What is included

  • Prior tax analysis to choose how to operate (S.R.L., S.A. or individual with business activity)
  • Registration with the Tax Administration and economic activities
  • Initial obligations: electronic invoicing, company e-mail and beneficial-ownership registry
  • Accounting system and books from day one
  • Bookkeeping and tax returns from the first month

When to talk to us

Before signing the deed: the company type, capital, activity and partners determine later obligations and costs. If your company already exists, we can also bring it up to date.

Frequently asked questions

Do you draft the incorporation deed?

The deed is granted by a notary public. We coordinate with your notary and handle tax registration and bookkeeping.

What is the RTBF?

It is Costa Rica's Registry of Transparency and Beneficial Owners, which identifies the people who control a company. We check current deadlines and penalties for each case.

S.R.L. or S.A.?

It depends on the number of partners, how you want to handle transfers of ownership and your tax planning. We go through it in a consultation.
