Financial Projections and Analysis for Better Decisions

A good financial model turns assumptions into decisions: how much to invest, how much to finance and when. We build clear projections with documented assumptions and scenarios you can understand and discuss with your bank or partners.

What is included

  • Projected cash flow
  • Multi-year projected financial statements
  • Scenario and break-even analysis
  • Investment project evaluation
  • Feasibility analysis for credit applications

When it makes sense

Before opening a new business or branch, buying property or equipment, applying for credit or accepting an investor. The model is built on your real numbers, so it works best when your books are up to date.

Frequently asked questions

Can I present it to a bank?

Yes, as long as it is based on real accounting information. We prepare it with the structure financial institutions usually request.

What information do I need?

Recent financial statements, income and cost detail, the investment plan and the assumptions you use. We help organize and validate them.
