Certified Public Accountant (CPA) Certifications in Costa Rica

A CPA certification is a statement issued by a Costa Rican Certified Public Accountant (Contador Público Autorizado) about verifiable financial information of a person or company. Banks, public entities, partners and investors ask for it as professional support. At GAMA Asesores Contables it is issued by CPA Gustavo Monge A., based on evidence and in line with Law No. 7527 and applicable professional standards.

Certifications we prepare

  • Income certifications for individuals and independent professionals
  • Income and sales certifications for companies
  • Certification of financial statements and account balances
  • Certifications for credit applications, procurement and other third-party procedures
  • English versions for foreign entities and procedures abroad

When you need one

Typically requested when applying for credit, taking part in a procurement process, reporting to a partner or investor, or answering a request from an institution. The receiving entity defines the exact scope, so it is best to confirm it before hiring.

A certification is only issued when there is enough support: accounting records, bank statements, filed tax returns and supporting documents. We do not issue certifications without evidence.

Frequently asked questions

Which documents do I need for an income certification?

Usually the tax returns filed with the Tax Administration, issued electronic invoices, bank statements for the period and, where applicable, financial statements. Additional documents may be requested depending on the case.

How long does it take?

It depends on the condition of your records and the scope the receiving entity asks for. With complete, organized documentation it is much faster; we confirm the timeline when quoting.

Can the certification be issued in English?

Yes. We prepare English versions for foreign entities and procedures abroad.
